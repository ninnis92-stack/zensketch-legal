ZenSketch Support

ZenSketch is a journal and sketchbook for drawing and writing on paper-style pages, on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the paywall and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Sync across devices. Sync is part of ZenSketch Plus. Sign in to iCloud on each device and make sure iCloud is allowed for ZenSketch in Settings.
  • Keep a copy of your journals. Open Your data from the Home screen and choose Back up all to save a backup file to Files. Choose Restore on another device to add it to that library. Restoring never overwrites what is already there.
  • Dictation or Smart Canvas does not start. Allow microphone, speech recognition, camera or photos for ZenSketch in iOS Settings.
  • More room on the page. Hide the tool dock or the side panel with the small buttons beside them. They bring the tools back in the same place.

Privacy and data

Your pages are stored on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the public ZenSketch Legal GitHub repository. Please do not post passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

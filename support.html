SketchTome Studio Support

SketchTome Studio is a sketchbook for drawing on paper-style pages, on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo.

Common questions

  • Restore a purchase. Open the paywall and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Cancel or change Plus. In the app, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also go to the iPhone or iPad Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Lifelong Pro is a single payment and has nothing to cancel.
  • Sync across devices. Sync is part of SketchTome Studio Plus. Sign in to iCloud on each device and make sure iCloud is allowed for SketchTome Studio in Settings.
  • Keep a copy of your sketchbooks. Open Settings from the Home screen and choose Back up all to save a backup file to Files. Choose Restore on another device to add it to that library. Restoring never overwrites what is already there.
  • Camera Sketch does not start. Allow the camera for SketchTome Studio in iOS Settings. Choosing a photo from your library needs no permission.
  • Camera Sketch says to wait. The free version includes two Camera Sketches, then one more each week. Pro has no limit.
  • Choose a colour. Tap the colour dot in the tool bar for eight inks, or the colour wheel after them for any colour. Text and stamps have the same choice in their panel.
  • Find a tool. Tap the three dots (Actions) and type what you want, such as layers, mirror or export. The Guide in Settings explains every feature.
  • More room on the page. Hide the tool dock or the side panel with the small buttons beside them. They bring the tools back in the same place.

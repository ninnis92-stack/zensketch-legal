ZenSketch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

ZenSketch is a journal and sketchbook app by Naheem Innis. This policy explains what ZenSketch does with information.

In short: We do not track you across apps or websites. ZenSketch has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer does not run a server that receives your pages. Your journals live on your device.

Your pages

Journals, pages, drawings, text and covers are stored on your device. ZenSketch does not upload them to the developer. If you use iCloud Backup for your device, ZenSketch's data is part of that backup, which Apple manages.

Camera and photos

Smart Canvas can use your camera, or a photo you choose from your library, to make a sketch outline. Journal covers can use a photo you choose. The outline is made on your device with Apple's Core Image framework. Photos are not sent to the developer. A photo you choose as a cover is stored with that journal on your device.

Handwriting search

Search reads your pages using Apple's Vision framework on your device.

Dictation and the microphone

When you turn on dictation, ZenSketch listens through the microphone while dictation is active and turns your speech into text on the page. Speech recognition runs on your device whenever your device supports it. If it does not, Apple may process the audio using its speech-recognition service under Apple's privacy terms. ZenSketch does not save audio recordings.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on sync with ZenSketch Plus, your journals sync through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. The developer cannot see or access that data. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to it. Without sync, nothing leaves your device.

Backup files

You can save a backup file of your journals to Files or send it to another device. You decide where it goes. Restoring a backup adds to your library and never overwrites it.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. ZenSketch learns which purchases you own so it can unlock them. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

ZenSketch does not track you, show advertising, or include analytics or other third-party SDKs. It does not collect data types that need to be disclosed in the App Store's privacy labels.

Deleting your data

You can delete a page or a journal in the app. Deleting ZenSketch from your device removes its local data. If you used sync, you can manage or delete ZenSketch's iCloud data in Settings under your Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.

Children

ZenSketch does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public ZenSketch Legal GitHub repository. Please do not post passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.
